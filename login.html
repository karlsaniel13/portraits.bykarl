How to Order a Portrait

Direct message us on this account and fill up these details:

  • Name:
  • Size of the Portrait: (Small or Large) and the orientation (Landscape or Portrait)
  • Photo of the Portrait that I would be doing:
  • Place where the Portrait is going to be shipped:
  • Additionals:

Important Reminders:

  • GCash payments are only acceptable.
  • A down payment costing half of the actual price should be paid first to ensure the order. Send the screenshot of your payment to officially start the portrait.
  • After the down payment, no refunds are allowed.
  • The portrait would be finished in a matter of 2-3 days depending on the difficulty of the portrait. To ensure the portrait would arrive on time, I would suggest you order it a week or two before the actual date.
  • 2 orders of portraits are only accepted every week to ensure the portraits are going to be done on time with quality.
